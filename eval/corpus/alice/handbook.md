# Team Handbook

Welcome to the team. This handbook explains how we work together. If something is missing, ask in the team channel and then add it here.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your own time zone. Outside the core hours everybody organises their day freely. Working from home is the default, and the office is open for those who prefer it. Please tell the team in the morning thread when you are unavailable.

## Vacation and time off

Every employee has 25 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests are made in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day, and the team lead approves them within three working days. Public holidays do not count as vacation days. Unused days can be carried over until the end of March of the next year. Sick leave needs no request: write to your lead before the start of the working day, and from the fourth day a doctor's note is required.

## Equipment

New colleagues receive a laptop, a screen and a headset. Laptops are backed up automatically once a week to the company storage, so important work should still be pushed to the repository every day. Report a broken device to the IT desk, which usually answers within one working day. Lost equipment must be reported immediately.

## Accounts and passwords

Accounts are created by the IT desk on the first day. To reset your password, open the login page, choose "forgot password" and follow the link in the email. Passwords must have at least 14 characters, and the password manager is mandatory. Never share a password in chat. Multi-factor authentication has to be enabled within the first week.

## Meetings

Keep meetings short and give them an agenda. Weekly planning is on Monday at 10:30. Decisions are written down in the meeting notes the same day so that people who were absent can follow them.
