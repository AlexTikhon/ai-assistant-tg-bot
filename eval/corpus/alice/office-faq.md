# Office and Equipment FAQ

## Wi-Fi in the office

The guest network is called "Guest" and its password changes every month; it is printed on the board at reception. Employees use the secured network with their normal account. The office network does not work for devices at home, and the VPN is only needed to reach the internal tools from outside.

## Printers

The printer on the second floor is set up for colour and double-sided printing. Print jobs that are not collected are deleted after one hour. For a paper jam, open the side cover, pull out the sheet slowly in the direction of the paper path and close the cover again. If the printer still shows an error, write to the IT desk.

## Headsets and monitors

Headsets connect by Bluetooth. Hold the button on the earcup until the light blinks, then choose the headset in the Bluetooth settings of the laptop. Monitors are adjustable in height; ask facilities if you need a different stand. Please switch off monitors when you leave in the evening.

## Visitors

Visitors register at reception and receive a badge. They must be accompanied at all times. Parcels are delivered to the reception desk, and you will get a message when something arrives for you.

## Kitchen and meeting rooms

Clean up after yourself and run the dishwasher when it is full. Meeting rooms are booked in the calendar and released automatically if nobody checks in within ten minutes.
