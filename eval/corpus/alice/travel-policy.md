# Business Travel Policy

Travel must be approved by the team lead before anything is booked.

## Booking

Book flights and trains through the travel portal so that the company rates apply. Economy class is the standard, and a longer flight above six hours may be booked in premium economy with approval. Book as early as possible, because prices rise sharply in the last two weeks. Changes after booking cost money and should be avoided.

## Hotels

The hotel budget is 140 euros per night in most cities and 190 euros in the capitals with high prices. Hotels should be close to the meeting place and have a cancellation option. Breakfast is reimbursed only if it is part of the room rate. Private upgrades are paid by the traveller.

## Per diem and expenses

Meals while travelling are reimbursed as described in the expense guide. Local transport, such as taxis from the station, is reimbursed with a receipt. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Hand in all receipts within two weeks after the trip.

## Insurance and safety

The company travel insurance covers medical costs and lost luggage on business trips. Share your itinerary with your lead and keep your phone charged. In an emergency call the number on the insurance card first and then the team lead.

## Combining business and private travel

A private stay can be added before or after a business trip. The extra days are paid privately and do not count as business days. The company only pays the part of the flight that a direct business trip would have cost.
