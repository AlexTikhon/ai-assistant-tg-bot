# PurrFeed 3 Automatic Cat Feeder: User Manual

The PurrFeed 3 releases a measured portion of dry food at scheduled times, so your cat is fed on time even when nobody is home. It holds up to 2 kilograms of food in its hopper and runs on four D batteries or on the included power adapter.

## Setting up portions and schedules

Open the PurrFeed app and choose a meal slot. Each slot has a time and a portion size between 1 and 12 portions, where one portion is roughly 6 grams. Up to eight meals per day can be scheduled. Changes take effect at the next meal. The feeder keeps its schedule in memory, so it continues to work during a power cut or when the internet is down.

## Wi-Fi pairing

Hold the pairing button on the back for five seconds until the front light blinks blue. The feeder only supports 2.4 GHz networks, so make sure your phone is connected to a 2.4 GHz network before starting the pairing in the app. If the light turns red, the password was rejected: move closer to the router and try again. Pairing a new phone does not remove the schedule.

## Clearing a jammed hopper

If the feeder turns but no food comes out, the hopper is probably jammed by a clump of food or by a piece that is too large. Clearing a jammed hopper takes a few minutes: unplug the feeder, remove the lid and lift out the food, then remove the rotating paddle by turning it a quarter turn counter-clockwise. Pick out the stuck pieces, refit the paddle, refill the hopper with fresh dry kibble and run a test portion from the app. Do not use semi-moist or wet food, which clumps and causes most jams.

## Error codes

The display shows a code when something needs attention.

E-4012 means the battery is low. Replace all four batteries at the same time, or plug in the adapter. The feeder still dispenses the next meal on the remaining charge.

E-4020 means the paddle motor is blocked. Follow the instructions for a jammed hopper above.

E-4031 means the clock lost its time because the batteries ran flat while the adapter was unplugged. Reconnect to the app to sync the time.

## Resetting the device

To reset the feeder to factory settings, hold the pairing button for fifteen seconds until the light blinks white three times. This erases the schedule and the Wi-Fi settings but not your account in the app.

## Cleaning

Clean the hopper and the paddle every two weeks. Step one: unplug the feeder and empty the hopper. Step two: wash the lid, hopper and paddle by hand in warm water with a mild soap. Step three: dry every part completely before putting it back, because moisture makes the food stick. Never put the base unit in water. Warning: the lid and the paddle are not dishwasher safe, and heat will warp them so that the lid no longer seals.
