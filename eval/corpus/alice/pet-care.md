# Cat Care Notes

Notes on keeping our two cats healthy, collected from the vet and from experience.

## Feeding schedule

Adult cats do best with two or three small meals per day instead of one big bowl. Our cats get breakfast at 07:00 and dinner at 18:30, and a small snack at midday on workdays. Fresh water must always be available, and the water bowl is washed every day. Changing the brand of food should be done slowly over a week, mixing a little more of the new food into the old one each day, otherwise the stomach protests.

## Types of food

Dry kibble is convenient and good for the teeth, wet food provides more water and is useful for cats that drink too little. A mix of both works well. Treats should stay below ten percent of the daily calories. Milk is not a good drink for adult cats, since most of them cannot digest lactose.

## Vet visits

A check-up once a year is enough for a healthy adult cat, and vaccinations are renewed every year or every three years depending on the vaccine. Weigh the cat every month and write the number down; a slow weight change is often the first sign that something is wrong. Call the vet if a cat refuses food for more than a day.

## Travel and holidays

When we are away for a weekend, an automatic dispenser plus a neighbour who checks in every day is enough. For longer trips the cats stay with a friend. Always leave the vet's phone number and the insurance details with whoever looks after them.
