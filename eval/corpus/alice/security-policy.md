# Information Security Policy

This policy applies to everyone who works with company data.

## Passwords and authentication

Use the company password manager for every account and let it generate the passwords. Passwords are not changed on a schedule, but they must be changed at once after a suspected leak. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the code repository and the cloud console. Hardware keys are preferred over authenticator apps, and text message codes are not allowed.

## Devices

All laptops must use full disk encryption and a screen lock after five minutes. Install system updates within a week. Personal devices may only be used for email and chat, and never to store customer data. A lost device must be reported to the security desk within one hour so that it can be wiped remotely.

## Phishing

Be careful with unexpected links and attachments, even when the sender looks familiar. Check the real address of the sender and hover over links before clicking. Report suspicious messages with the report button of the mail client; do not forward them to colleagues. A simulated phishing test runs once per quarter and the results are used for training, not for blame.

## Data handling

Customer data stays in the production systems and is not copied to laptops or spreadsheets. Exports are only allowed with a ticket that names the purpose, and they are deleted after thirty days. Backups of customer data are encrypted and the keys are kept by the security team.

## Incidents

Anyone who suspects a security incident writes immediately to the security channel and does not try to investigate alone. The security lead decides about containment and about informing customers or authorities within the legal deadlines.
