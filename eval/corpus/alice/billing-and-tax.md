# Billing, Expenses and Tax Guide

This guide covers how we invoice clients, how expenses are recorded and when the tax paperwork is due. It is maintained by the finance team.

## Invoicing

Invoices are issued on the last working day of each month and are payable within 30 days. Every invoice shows the net amount, the VAT line and the total. Late payments receive a reminder after 7 days and a second reminder after 21 days. Interest on overdue invoices is charged at 4 percent per year.

## Refund policy

Refunds are issued within 14 days of the request when the service was not delivered as agreed. The money goes back to the original payment method, and the credit note is sent by email the same day. Requests older than 90 days are reviewed individually by the finance lead. Partial refunds are possible when only a part of the service was affected.

## Expenses

Employees submit expenses through the expense tool together with a photo of the receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day. Train tickets are reimbursed in second class, and flights need approval from a manager before booking. Capital expenditure above 1,000 euros, such as laptops or screens, must be approved by the finance lead and is recorded as an asset instead of an expense.

## VAT

VAT returns are filed quarterly. The standard rate is 21 percent and the reduced rate for books and some food products is 9 percent. Invoices to clients outside the country are issued without VAT when a valid VAT number of the client is on file.

## Tax deadlines

The annual tax return is due on April 15 of the following year. Quarterly advance payments are due on the 15th of January, April, July and October. If the paperwork cannot be completed in time, an extension request must reach the tax office before the deadline, never after it. Keep all receipts and invoices for seven years.
