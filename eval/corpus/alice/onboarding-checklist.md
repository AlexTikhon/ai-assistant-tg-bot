# Onboarding Checklist for New Colleagues

A short list for the first weeks. Your buddy will go through it with you.

## Day one

Pick up the laptop from the IT desk and sign in. Set up the password manager and the second factor. Join the team channel and the operations channel. Read the handbook and the security policy; both are short.

## First week

Get access to the code repository and run the project setup described in the setup guide. Shadow a colleague on the on-call rotation, without taking part in it yet. Meet the product owner and the support lead for thirty minutes each. Agree on your goals for the first three months with your team lead.

## First month

Pick up a small ticket and bring it to production. Present what you learned about the product at the Friday demo. Plan your first vacation together with your lead, so that important releases are covered, and enter it in the HR portal in good time. Give feedback on this checklist, since it is improved with every new colleague.

## Contacts

The IT desk answers questions about devices and accounts. The finance team answers questions about expenses and invoices. HR answers everything about contracts, salary dates and benefits. When in doubt, ask your buddy.
